Delete your account

You can request deletion of your MokshaVoice account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. 1

    Email us from the address you'd like us to reply to, including the phone number registered on your MokshaVoice account.

  2. 2

    We verify the request is coming from the account owner.

  3. 3

    Your account and personal data are deleted within 30 days, and we confirm by email once it's done.

Send your request

Email admin@waytomoksha.org with the subject line "Delete my account" and your registered phone number.

Email admin@waytomoksha.org

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: phone number, display name
  • Your dream submissions: voice recordings and session messages
  • Your session and consultation history
  • Your push notification token

What we may retain: billing records tied to past purchases, where required for tax or legal compliance — these aren't personally identifying beyond what Apple, Google, or Stripe already require for payment records.

For the full picture of what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
