01Overview

MokshaVoice (“we,” “us,” “our”) operates the MokshaVoice mobile app for iOS and Android. The app lets you record a spoken description of a dream and have it reviewed and interpreted by a human decoder, analyzer, or mentor — plus related features like session chat, push notifications, and a premium subscription.

This policy covers the data we collect through the app and our backend services, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the controls you have over it.

02Information we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run the service:

We don't collect: your precise location, contacts, photos outside what you explicitly attach, or browsing activity outside the app.

03How we use it

  • Deliver the core service: routing your recorded dream to an available decoder/analyzer and returning their response
  • Verify your identity via SMS one-time password at sign-in
  • Track your monthly quota (5 free / 15 premium dream analyses) and enforce subscription limits
  • Send push notifications about session activity
  • Process and validate subscription purchases and renewals
  • Diagnose bugs and crashes, and keep the service secure

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your dream content to train third-party AI models or for advertising.

04Who sees your dream submissions

Dream recordings and the text of your session are visible to the staff involved in your session: the decoder or analyzer assigned to review it, and, where applicable, a mentor handling a follow-up consultation. These are people operating the service, not the general public, and they're bound to use what you share only to provide your analysis.

We don't share your dream content with other customers, publish it, or disclose it to outside parties except where Section 5 or the law requires it.

06Data retention

We keep account and session data for as long as your account is active, so you can see your past dream analyses and conversation history. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data and dream content within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain billing records for tax or legal purposes.

07Your rights and choices

  • Access or export: ask us for a copy of the data tied to your account
  • Correction: update your display name any time in the app's profile screen
  • Deletion: request your account and associated data be deleted, via the contact below
  • Notifications: turn off push notifications at any time in your device settings
  • Subscription: manage or cancel your subscription through the App Store, Play Store, or Stripe customer portal, depending on how you subscribed

08Children's privacy

MokshaVoice is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

09Security

Voice recordings and account data are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls limiting who can reach them. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

10Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
